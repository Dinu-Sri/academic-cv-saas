LaTeX dominates academic CV formatting because it produces typographic quality that word processors cannot replicate. Search committees at research universities, fellowship review panels, and journal editors recognize LaTeX-formatted documents by their precise kerning, proper ligatures, and consistent mathematical typesetting — qualities that signal scholarly competence before a single word is read. This guide explains why LaTeX remains the standard and how to access its benefits without learning to code. For complementary guidance, see [font selection and academic CV typography](/blog/font-selection-academic-cv-typography).

LaTeX is a document preparation system that separates content from formatting. You write your CV content in plain text with lightweight markup commands, and LaTeX compiles it into a precisely formatted PDF. This separation provides 3 advantages for academic CVs: consistent formatting across all entries regardless of length, automatic handling of bibliographic references through BibTeX, and mathematical typesetting that word processors cannot match. For guidance on structuring the content that fills LaTeX templates, see [how to write an academic CV — the complete guide](/blog/how-to-write-academic-cv-complete-guide). The output quality stems from LaTeX's implementation of the TeX typesetting algorithm, which optimizes line breaks, hyphenation, and spacing across entire paragraphs rather than line by line.

## What Typographic Advantages Does LaTeX Provide Over Word Processors?

LaTeX provides 5 typographic advantages over word processors for academic CVs: proper ligatures, optically spaced margins, consistent mathematical typesetting, automatic hyphenation with language-specific patterns, and font kerning optimized for scholarly text. These advantages produce documents that appear professionally published rather than word-processed.

Ligatures — the joining of character pairs like "fi," "fl," and "ff" into single typographic units — improve readability and signal typographic quality. Word processors may or may not use ligatures depending on the font and settings. LaTeX uses them consistently with all professional fonts. Mathematical typesetting in LaTeX handles inline equations, displayed formulas, and complex notation with spacing rules developed over 40 years of scholarly publishing practice. For academics in mathematics, physics, engineering, and quantitative social sciences, this capability makes LaTeX essential rather than optional.

## How Do LaTeX CV Templates Reduce Formatting Burden?

LaTeX CV templates provide pre-configured document classes that handle section formatting, font selection, margin settings, and spacing automatically. You fill in the content fields and the template generates a consistently formatted PDF. Popular academic CV templates include moderncv, which provides elegant sans-serif designs, and AltaCV, which produces clean two-column layouts with configurable color schemes.

Template systems eliminate the need to manually position elements, adjust spacing between sections, or maintain consistent heading styles. When you update a template-based LaTeX CV — adding a publication, changing your address, or reordering sections — the template recalculates all layout parameters automatically. In a word processor, the same update might require manual adjustment of page breaks, section spacing, and alignment across multiple pages. This automation saves hours over the lifetime of an academic CV that grows from 4 pages as a PhD student to 25 pages as a senior faculty member.

## What Are the Version Control Benefits of LaTeX for CVs?

LaTeX source files are plain text, which means they work with version control systems like Git. Every change to your CV becomes a tracked commit with a timestamp and description. You can revert to previous versions, compare changes between versions, and maintain a complete history of your CV's evolution. This capability matters for academics who maintain multiple CV versions for different applications and need to track which version was submitted to which institution.

Version control also enables collaboration. Multiple researchers can contribute to a shared LaTeX CV or publication list through standard Git workflows. For research groups that maintain shared publication databases in BibTeX format, version control ensures that every group member's CV references the most current version of each citation. Word processor documents, by contrast, create binary files that cannot be meaningfully version-controlled, leading to the familiar "CV_final_v3_revised_Oct2024.docx" file proliferation that version control eliminates.

## What Are the Limitations of LaTeX That Academics Should Know?

LaTeX has 3 limitations relevant to academic CVs: a learning curve for customization beyond template defaults, limited support for certain non-Latin scripts without additional packages, and difficulty with complex table layouts that require manual coding. These limitations rarely affect standard academic CV formatting but may matter for scholars in fields with specific typographic requirements or those who need highly customized layouts.

The learning curve varies by task. Filing a template with content requires no LaTeX knowledge. Modifying template colors, fonts, or section ordering requires familiarity with LaTeX commands but can be accomplished by following documentation. Creating a custom document class from scratch requires significant LaTeX expertise. Most academics operate at the first or second level — using existing templates or making minor modifications — and find the trade-off between learning investment and output quality favorable.
