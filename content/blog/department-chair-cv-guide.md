Department chair CVs document a career phase where administrative leadership joins research and teaching as a primary professional activity. Search committees evaluating candidates for chair positions, deanships, or other leadership roles look for evidence of organizational management, personnel supervision, budget responsibility, and strategic planning — competencies that standard academic CV sections do not capture.

Create an "Academic Leadership" section that appears prominently on the first or second page. Document each leadership role with the position title, institution, dates, scope of responsibility, and key accomplishments. For the department chair role: "Chair, Department of Psychology, 2022-2025. Supervised 22 faculty and 8 staff. Managed $3.2M annual budget. Led curriculum revision resulting in 3 new degree programs. Increased external research funding by 40% over 3 years." For committee leadership: "Chair, University Promotion and Tenure Committee, 2024-2025. Reviewed 45 cases. Revised evaluation criteria to recognize public scholarship and community-engaged research." These entries communicate administrative competence through specific metrics.
