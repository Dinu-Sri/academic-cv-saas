Education faculty CVs document a career that bridges academic research and professional practice. Unlike most academic disciplines, education faculty positions often require — or strongly prefer — K-12 teaching experience alongside scholarly credentials. For comparison, see the [social sciences academic CV guide](/blog/social-sciences-academic-cv-guide) which shares mixed-methods research conventions. Hiring committees evaluate candidates on teaching experience (both K-12 and higher education), scholarly contributions to educational research, and engagement with school communities and educational policy.

Lead with a combined profile that synthesizes your practitioner and scholarly identities: "Associate Professor of Curriculum and Instruction with 12 years of middle school science teaching experience. Research focuses on inquiry-based STEM instruction in underserved schools. $2.1M in funded research. 3-time recipient of school partnership excellence awards." The teaching experience section should include both K-12 and higher education roles, with K-12 entries including school district, grade levels, subjects taught, and student achievement data. For formatting guidance, see [how to document teaching experience on an academic CV](/blog/teaching-experience-academic-cv-guide). Service entries should document school partnerships, community engagement, and practitioner-oriented outreach — activities that education faculty colleagues value as evidence of bridging research and practice. For service documentation, see [how to present professional service and committee work](/blog/professional-service-committee-work-academic-cv).
