# Internal Linking Plan

## Link Architecture

### Hub-and-Spoke Model
- **Hub pages**: Pillar articles (P1-01, P5-01) link to ALL cluster articles in their pillar
- **Spoke pages**: Cluster articles link back to their pillar + 2-3 related clusters
- **Cross-pillar links**: Each article includes 1-2 links to articles in other pillars

### Mandatory Links (Every Article)
1. Link to pillar article of same pillar
2. Link to CVScholar tool (CTA: "Build Your Academic CV Free")
3. Link to 1 related article in same pillar
4. Link to 1 article in different pillar (topical bridge)

### Link Placement Rules
- First link within first 200 words (to pillar or most relevant cluster)
- CTA link after first major section
- Related articles section at bottom (3 links)
- Contextual in-text links (2-4 per 1000 words)

## Navigation Pages
- `/blog` — Shows all articles with category/tag filters
- `/blog/category/{name}` — Category archive (maps to pillars)
- `/blog/tag/{name}` — Tag archive (cross-cutting topics)

## Anchor Text Guidelines
- Vary anchor text (don't always use exact title)
- Use descriptive phrases: "learn how to list publications" → P3-01
- Avoid generic "click here" or "read more"
- Include long-tail keywords in anchors where natural
