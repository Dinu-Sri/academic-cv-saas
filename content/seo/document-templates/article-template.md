## Content Guidelines

### Word Count
- Standard articles: 1,500-2,500 words
- Pillar articles: 3,000-4,000 words
- FAQ articles: 800-1,200 words

### Tone
- Authoritative but approachable
- Second person ("you/your")
- Active voice preferred
- No jargon without explanation

### SEO Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, meta description
- [ ] 2-3 secondary keywords used naturally
- [ ] At least 1 internal link to pillar article
- [ ] At least 2 internal links to related cluster articles
- [ ] CTA section linking to CVScholar registration
- [ ] Meta description under 160 characters
- [ ] All H2s contain relevant keywords (not forced)

### Categories (use exactly one)
- CV Writing Guide (P1)
- Career Stage Guide (P2)
- CV Sections (P3)
- Field-Specific (P4)
- Job Market (P5)
