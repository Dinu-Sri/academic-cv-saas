## Publishing Rules

1. **Pillar First**: Never publish a cluster before its pillar hub is live
2. **Breadth Before Depth**: Spread across pillars each week; don't publish 3 articles from the same pillar in one week
3. **Link Before Publish**: Ensure all mandatory internal links exist before publishing (link to pillar hub, 1-2 same-pillar clusters, 1 cross-pillar bridge)
4. **Update Pillar**: After publishing each cluster, add its link to the pillar hub article
5. **CTA Required**: Every article ends with a CVScholar CTA
6. **Entity Coverage**: Track which entity attributes each article covers; flag underserved attributes
