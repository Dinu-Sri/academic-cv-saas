CVScholar is operated by **Clossyan Technologies (Pvt) Ltd** ("CVScholar", "we", "our", or "us"). This Cookie Policy explains how and why we use cookies and similar technologies on **https://cvscholar.com**.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site owners.

Similar technologies include:
- **Local storage** — browser storage for preferences and session data
- **Pixels/tags** — small images or code snippets that track page visits
- **Scripts** — JavaScript that runs analytics and feature tracking

## 2. Cookie Categories We Use

### Essential Cookies (Always Active)
These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They include:
- Session cookies for authentication and staying logged in
- CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery) protection tokens
- Load balancing and security cookies

**Examples:** `PHPSESSID`, `_token`, `XSRF-TOKEN`

### Functional Cookies
These enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as:
- Remembering your preferred CV template or website theme
- Saving your editor preferences (font size, last-used section)
- Storing your cookie consent preferences

### Analytics Cookies
These help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously:
- **Google Analytics** — page views, session duration, traffic sources, user journeys. IP anonymization is enabled. No personally identifiable information is sent.
- **PostHog** — feature usage patterns, session analysis, event tracking. Used exclusively for product improvement. Client IP geo-resolution is disabled by default.

### Marketing Cookies
These may be set through our site by advertising partners:
- We do not currently run third-party advertising networks or retargeting pixels
- If we introduce marketing cookies in the future, we will update this policy and request your explicit consent

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to track visitor interactions. We have enabled IP anonymization. Data is processed in accordance with Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. How to Manage Cookies

### Cookie Consent Banner
When you first visit CVScholar, a cookie consent banner appears allowing you to:
- **Accept All** — enable all cookie categories
- **Reject All** — only essential cookies remain active
- **Customize** — choose which categories to enable individually

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the website footer.

### Browser Settings
You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- View and delete existing cookies
- Block all or third-party cookies
- Clear cookies when you close the browser

Instructions for common browsers:
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

Note: Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of CVScholar.

## 7. International Users

### European Economic Area (EEA) / EU GDPR
Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and ePrivacy Directive, we:
- Obtain explicit consent before setting non-essential cookies
- Provide granular control over cookie categories
- Allow withdrawal of consent at any time
- Maintain records of consent choices

### United Kingdom (UK GDPR)
The UK GDPR and Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) apply similar requirements to the EU. Our cookie consent mechanism complies with both UK and EU standards.

### United States (California CCPA/CPRA)
Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA, California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell data)
- Request deletion of personal information

Our cookie consent banner provides California residents with a clear opt-out mechanism for non-essential cookies.

### Other Jurisdictions
We aim to provide a consistent, privacy-respecting experience for all users regardless of location. Our default cookie settings are privacy-preserving, and non-essential cookies require active opt-in.
