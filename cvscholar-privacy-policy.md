# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2 April 2026

CVScholar is operated by **Clossyan Technologies (Pvt) Ltd** ("CVScholar", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit **https://cvscholar.com**, create an account, use our academic CV builder, purchase a paid plan, contact support, or otherwise use our services.

By accessing or using CVScholar, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to our website, web application, public CV sharing features, customer support channels, payment-related interactions, and any related services we provide.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

### a. Information you provide directly
- Full name
- Email address
- Username and account login credentials
- Profile details you choose to provide
- Academic CV content, including education, employment, publications, grants, awards, teaching, supervision, conferences, editorial roles, and similar academic information
- Messages sent through our contact form, support channels, or other communications
- Preferences and settings you configure within your account

### b. Information imported from third-party services
If you choose to use optional integrations or import tools, we may receive information you authorize from third-party services such as ORCID, Google Scholar, or similar academic data sources. This may include profile details, publications, employment history, education history, citations, and related academic metadata.

### c. Billing and payment information
When you purchase a paid plan, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment processor or gateway. We may receive limited transaction-related information such as:
- Payment status
- Transaction reference
- Subscription status
- Billing country
- Partial card or payment method details where provided by the processor

We do **not** intentionally store full card numbers, CVV numbers, or full banking credentials on our own servers unless explicitly stated otherwise.

### d. Automatically collected information
When you use our website or services, we may automatically collect:
- IP address
- Device information
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referring URLs
- Session and activity logs
- Dates and times of access
- Pages viewed and features used
- Diagnostic, crash, and performance data
- Cookie and similar tracking information

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:
- To create and manage your account
- To provide the CVScholar service and its features
- To generate, display, format, export, and store your CVs and related documents
- To provide public CV links when you choose to enable sharing
- To process subscriptions, payments, billing, renewals, and account plan changes
- To provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- To maintain security, monitor misuse, and prevent fraud or unauthorized access
- To improve product features, performance, and user experience
- To send service-related notices, security alerts, billing updates, and administrative communications
- To comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and contractual obligations
- To enforce our Terms of Use and protect our rights, users, and platform

## 4. Public CV Links and Visibility

CVScholar allows users to create shareable public links for CVs. If you enable a public link, the content associated with that link may be viewed by anyone who has access to the link. You are responsible for deciding what information to include in any CV that you choose to share publicly.

You may disable or remove a public CV link through your account controls where that functionality is available.

- Authentication and account session management
- Security and fraud prevention
- Saving user preferences
- Analytics and performance measurement
- Improving product functionality
